## Czech and Slovak
- **The blueprint block is a depth map** — a modrotlač motif as the tile, the carved printing block as the relief. UNESCO 2018. https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/blueprint-resist-block-printing-and-indigo-dyeing-in-europe-01365 · ÚĽUV image licence unstated: redraw or ask. M
- **Comenius's spectacles** — one plate with the note: look parallel, then crossed, and the depth inverts; *Labyrinth of the World* ch. IV, Delusion's spectacles. https://czech.mml.ox.ac.uk/static/labyrint/Achptr04.html S
- **Orbis Pictus numbered plates** — numbered parts of the hidden figure, key in Latin, Czech and English. https://www.gutenberg.org/files/28299/28299-h/28299-h.htm M
- **The Orloj day plate** — 365 saints on the clock's calendar dial; a name-day card with the name hidden in depth. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague_astronomical_clock S
- **Pattern-book tiles** — counted-thread motifs (Siebmacher 1597) as pixel tiles; Moravian embroidery fed on them. https://uluv.sk/?p=127093 M

## Catholic and classical
- **Vyšehrad doves** — the earliest Tree of Jesse (Vyšehrad Codex c. 1085), seven doves as hidden figures; redraw, Manuscriptorium licence unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vy%C5%A1ehrad_Codex M
- **Advent Jesse book, 24 plates** — one symbol per day, printable or today's door only; no streak, no notification. Advent begins 29 Nov 2026. M
- **Fourteen Stations in low relief** — for Lenten Fridays; the 1991 Scriptural Way. L
- **The Chartres path** — the 11-circuit labyrinth, path raised, walls sunk. The owner is called Chartres. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labyrinth S
- **Cosmati tiles** — guilloche and roundels in marble, porphyry and serpentine as procedural tiles. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmatesque S
- **"Schiacciato" slider** — Ghiberti's flattened relief as the name and the history of the depth-compression control. S
- **Relief shelf** — reliefs bake better than statues. Verified CC0/PD: Cleveland book-shaped reliquary c. 1000 (https://www.sketchfab.com/3d-models/1930741-book-shaped-reliquary-c54e6cbecf5e450d86052da35a86af6d), Cleveland Coptic ceiling panel 1954.799, SMK plaster casts (https://blog.smk.dk/ghost-in-the-scan-3d-scans-of-casts-in-the-smks-collections), Cleveland open-access API keyless with a CC0 filter (https://clevelandart.org/open-access). Bishop Norton tympanum is CC BY 4.0 (credit Digital Heritage). M

## Education and children
- **Pink tower in depth** — ten cubes as ten depth steps; the answer printed upside down (control of error). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montessori_sensorial_materials S
- **Fröbel gift plates** — forms from gifts 3 and 4, rebuilt with real blocks. S
- **Tangram paradox** — Dudeney's two monks; the 13 convex tangrams as a series. S
- **Blind-spot worksheet** — a child finds her blind spot (~13.5°) and works out her distance; the UW "virtual chinrest". https://news.cs.washington.edu/?p=28784 S

## Vision science and calibration
- **"How fine can you see depth", honestly labelled** — credit-card screen calibration, blind-spot distance, ASTEROID's staircase (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6396686), result as "X arcsec or better on this screen" with the retest band (×4.3) and the pixel floor printed; needs its own stimulus, not the integer-separation renderer. M
- **Depth limit in arcminutes** — the slider's ceiling stated in arcmin, warning before fusion breaks. S
- **The 7 % page** — anaglyphs and viewers help people who cannot free-fuse, not the stereoblind; they get the depth map, a tactile print and the description. Rembrandt's exotropic self-portraits (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2634283) as the consolation. S
- **Julesz's minutes** — a private on-device time-to-depth log on the ladder; the user's own curve over weeks. S
